## Identifier policy

The identifiers below are controlled installation labels proposed for the Pilot.
Actual BIO-EMS `siteCode`, `deviceId`, channel number, and Sensor serial number remain
`TBD — Commissioning` until uniqueness and physical labels are verified.

No serial number or channel may be reused within the Pilot. The same identifier must
appear on the cable label, Sensor label, controller termination record, platform
configuration, calibration evidence, and commissioning form.

## Placement approval

Final positions require a marked-up floor plan and customer approval. The survey must
avoid treating a door opening, evaporator discharge, direct sunlight, heater, wall
surface, or local obstruction as representative storage temperature unless the
approved mapping rationale explicitly requires that location.

For each cold room, positions A and B must be selected from the approved temperature
mapping or a documented engineering rationale. Warehouse zones must reflect usable
storage distribution and identified hot/cold risk, not equal spacing alone.
