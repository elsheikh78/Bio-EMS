# Entity Relationships

Version: 1.0

---

## Site

A Site contains:

- Assets
- Devices
- Users

Relationship

Site (1)

├── Assets (N)

├── Devices (N)

└── Users (N)

---

## Asset

An Asset belongs to one Site.

An Asset contains one or more Monitoring Points.

Asset (1)

└── Monitoring Points (N)

---

## Monitoring Point

A Monitoring Point belongs to one Asset.

A Monitoring Point contains one or more Sensors.

Monitoring Point (1)

└── Sensors (N)

---

## Device

A Device belongs to one Site.

A Device provides one or more Device Channels.

Device (1)

└── Device Channels (N)

---

## Device Channel

A Device Channel belongs to one Device.

A Device Channel is connected to one Sensor.

Device Channel (1)

├── Device (1)

└── Sensor (1)

---

## Sensor

A Sensor is connected to:

- one Monitoring Point
- one Device Channel

Sensor

├── Monitoring Point (1)

└── Device Channel (1)

---

## Telemetry

Telemetry belongs to one Sensor.

Stored in InfluxDB.

---

## Alarm

An Alarm belongs to one Sensor.

Generated by the Alarm Engine.

---

## Notification

Notifications are generated from Alarms.
