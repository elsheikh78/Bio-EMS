# Entity Responsibilities

## Site

Represents a monitored facility.

Examples

- Pharmaceutical Warehouse
- Blood Bank
- Hospital
- Factory

---

## Room

Represents a monitored physical room.

Examples

- Cold Room
- Freezer
- Clean Room
- Laboratory

---

## Monitoring Point

Represents a physical measurement location.

Examples

- Door
- Center
- Ceiling
- Floor
- Evaporator

---

## Sensor

Represents a measurement source.

Examples

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Door
- Pressure
- CO₂

---

## Device

Represents the hardware controller.

Examples

- ESP32
- ESP32-S3

A Device contains one or more Sensor Channels.

---

## Telemetry

Represents historical measurements.

Stored in InfluxDB.

---

## Alarm

Represents abnormal conditions generated by the Alarm Engine.

---

# Principles

Devices are hardware.

Sensors are measurement sources.

Monitoring Points are physical locations.

Telemetry belongs to Sensors.

Rooms never communicate directly with Devices.
