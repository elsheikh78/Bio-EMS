## Position review rules

For every point, confirm and record the effect of doors, evaporator discharge/return,
walls, ceiling, sunlight, heaters, drains, traffic, stored-product layout, cleaning,
condensation, physical damage, and maintenance access. A central or convenient point
is not automatically representative.

Cold-room A/B points require an approved temperature-mapping reference or a signed
engineering rationale. Dry Warehouse zones must reflect storage distribution and
identified hot/cold risk rather than equal spacing alone.

## Completeness gate

- [ ] both customer drawing revisions identified;
- [ ] both Site/area boundaries verified against the controlled Pilot scope;
- [ ] all 20 Map IDs appear exactly once on the drawings and in the schedules;
- [ ] every position has a dimension/grid reference and mounting intent;
- [ ] every position has mapping or engineering rationale;
- [ ] bias, access, hygiene, damage, and maintenance risks reviewed;
- [ ] drawing and photograph references cross-checked;
- [ ] changes to area count or Sensor quantity routed through change control;
- [ ] BIO-EMS engineering review completed;
- [ ] BIO EGYPT Quality approval completed.
