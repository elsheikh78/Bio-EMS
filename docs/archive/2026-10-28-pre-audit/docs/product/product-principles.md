# Product Principles

Version: 1.0

---

## Principle 1

Users interact with Assets, not Devices.

---

## Principle 2

Hardware is replaceable.

Business configuration is permanent.

---

## Principle 3

Monitoring Points represent physical locations.

---

## Principle 4

Sensors represent measurement sources.

---

## Principle 5

Telemetry represents incoming data.

Business decisions are made from processed information.

---

## Principle 6

Every architecture decision must improve scalability.

---

## Principle 7

Documentation is part of the product.

---

## Principle 8

Design always precedes implementation.
